# Typical price ranges

These are typical ranges only. The final price follows an in-person inspection.

- Oil change: $45 to $110, depending on oil type.
- Brake pads (per axle): $150 to $350.
- Brake pads and rotors (per axle): $350 to $700.
- Brake fluid flush: $90 to $150.
- Tire rotation and balance: $40 to $90.
- Battery replacement: $150 to $350 including installation.
- Check-engine scan: $60 to $100, applied to repairs if you proceed.

Prices vary by vehicle make, model, and parts chosen.
