# Services

Maple Street Auto offers the following services:

- Oil and filter changes (conventional, synthetic blend, full synthetic).
- Brake work: brake pad and rotor replacement, brake fluid flush, and brake inspections. Yes, we do brake work on most cars, SUVs and light trucks.
- Tire rotation, balancing, and flat repair.
- Battery testing and replacement.
- Check-engine-light scans and general inspections.
- Air conditioning checks and recharge.

We do not service diesel heavy-duty trucks, RVs, or motorcycles.
Our technicians do not give diagnoses over chat or phone. A vehicle must be inspected in person before we can say what is wrong.
