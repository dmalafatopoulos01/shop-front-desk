# Warranty

Repairs done at Maple Street Auto are covered by a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor, whichever comes first.

Brake pads and rotors installed by us are covered for 24 months / 24,000 miles.
The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, misuse, or work done by other shops afterward.
Keep your invoice; it is needed for any warranty claim. Bring the vehicle back and we will inspect it at no charge.
